How tour.jpg is built
=====================

Six snipping-tool captures of the game window, 3 across and 2 down, each with a
caption strip under it. Kept here so the sheet can be rebuilt when the panel
changes rather than reassembled by hand.

The captures are of the whole window, so every one of them carries 38 px of
title bar on top and 1 px of frame on the other three sides. That comes off
with a single crop, and it is the same crop for all six because the window was
not resized between them:

    -crop 1280x960+1+38

If you recapture at a different window size, check the frame before assuming
those numbers still hold -- one pixel out and every tile has a teal hairline
down its edge.

Needs ImageMagick 7 (the "magick" command). Run it from this folder in a POSIX
shell; on Windows that is Git Bash.

    BG="#0D1117"      # GitHub's dark canvas, so the sheet reads as one object
    EDGE="#30363D"    # a hairline, so a bright tile still has an edge
    TEXT="#C9D1D9"

    cell () {         # cell <capture> <caption> <output>
      magick "$1" -crop 1280x960+1+38 +repage -resize 780x585 \
          -bordercolor "$EDGE" -border 1 tile.png
      magick -size 782x40 xc:"$BG" -font Consolas -pointsize 19 -fill "$TEXT" \
          -gravity west -annotate +15+0 "$2" cap.png
      magick tile.png cap.png -append "$3"
    }

    cell 8.PNG  "Aim at a line and it says whose it is  --  surf_hades2 bonus 4"   a.png
    cell 13.PNG "Watching a replay, every line drawn  --  surf_colin_blaster_69000" b.png
    cell 9.PNG  "Graphs: energy across a run, every line  --  surf_hades2 bonus 4" c.png
    cell 10.PNG "Runs: tick what gets drawn  --  273 cached on surf_utopia"        d.png
    cell 11.PNG "Board: the real leaderboard, fetch what you want  --  surf_tropic" e.png
    cell 12.PNG "Maps: 2050 of them, and which ones you hold  --  surf_zoomathon"  f.png

    magick montage a.png b.png c.png d.png e.png f.png \
        -tile 3x2 -geometry +8+8 -background "$BG" grid.png

    magick grid.png -bordercolor "$BG" -border 12 \
        -strip -quality 88 -sampling-factor 4:4:4 -interlace Plane tour.jpg

    rm -f tile.png cap.png a.png b.png c.png d.png e.png f.png grid.png

Three things there are deliberate. 4:4:4 sampling keeps the thin coloured lines
from bleeding into each other -- JPEG's default halves the chroma resolution,
which is exactly the wrong thing to do to a picture that is mostly one-pixel
lines of saturated colour. And the top row is what you look at in the world
while the bottom row is the lists that feed it, so the sheet reads left to
right and top to bottom as an order of operations.

And every caption names its map. Somebody asked which map each tile was, which
is a fair question to have to ask twice and not at all after that. The map goes
last, after a "  --  ", and the body text gets trimmed to make room: the strip
is 782 px of Consolas at pointsize 19 with a 15 px inset, so about 71 characters
fit and 66 is the bound to write to. Past that the last word walks off the right
edge and nothing warns you -- check the rebuilt sheet, do not trust the count.

The result is about 2400x1300 and 800 KB. GitHub scales it down to the width of
the README column and the full size is one click away, which is why the small
text in the tiles is worth keeping legible.
